THE MUSIC FACTORY by Archit

Program Courses

THE MUSIC FACTORY offers five structured music programs designed for beginners, developing musicians, performers, aspiring creators, and working professionals. Each course focuses on practical learning, musical understanding, creativity, and personal expression.

?

1. FOUNDATION (Beginners)

The Foundation program is designed for absolute beginners who are starting their musical journey for the first time. It builds strong fundamentals in rhythm, melody, listening, practice habits, and basic performance skills. The course is suitable for children, teenagers, and adults with little or no prior musical training.

Course Objectives

• Develop a steady sense of beat and rhythm.
• Understand the basic language of music.
• Learn simple scales, chords, and melodies.
• Build daily practice habits.
• Gain confidence through beginner-level songs and activities.

Chapters

Chapter 1 - Music Begins Here

• What is music?
• Beat, rhythm, melody, and harmony
• Listening activities
• Instrument introduction
     • Posture and practice habits Chapter 2 - Finding the Pulse
• Tempo and metronome
• Clapping patterns
• Body rhythm activities
• Breath and finger coordination
     • Counting aloud Chapter 3 - My First Melody
• Musical alphabet
• Major scale
• Simple notation
• Melody singing and playing
     • Phrasing and dynamics Chapter 4 - Songs Start Here
• Major and minor chords
• Chord changes
• Basic accompaniment
• Simple progressions
• First complete songs

Chapter 5 - Ear Training Fun

• High and low sounds
• Loud and soft sounds
• Same and different sounds
• Echo singing and rhythm imitation
• Listening games

2. INTERMEDIATE

• Improve musical expression and phrasing.
• Strengthen rhythm and accompaniment skills.
• Learn to perform with other musicians.
• Develop listening awareness while playing or singing.
• Build confidence for public performance.

• Rhythm exercises

Chapter 2 - Singing Through the Instrument

• Articulation
• Dynamics
     • Emotional delivery Chapter 3 - Playing Together
• Ensemble listening
• Balance and blending
• Cueing and eye contact
• Starting and ending together
     • Group performance exercises Chapter 4 - Confidence on Stage
• Stage posture
• Microphone basics
• Audience interaction
• Handling mistakes

• Performance psychology

Chapter 5 - The Circle of Fifths Made Easy

• The musical clock
• Friendly chord families
• Key relationships
• Common song progressions
     • Chord family activities Chapter 6 - Ear Training Adventure
• Melody direction
• Rhythm imitation
• Chord mood recognition
• Phrase echo exercises
• Listening memory games

3. PERFORMANCE LAB

Course Objectives

• Prepare complete performances independently.
• Develop professional stage habits.
• Improve audience communication.
• Experience rehearsal and showcase preparation.
• Build confidence through public performance.

Chapters

Chapter 1 - Building a Performance

• Song selection
• Practice planning
• Memorization techniques
• Performance structure
     • Rehearsal scheduling Chapter 2 - The Stage Experience
• Stage entry and exit
• Body language
• Microphone handling
• Audience connection
     • Performance etiquette Chapter 3 - Studio and Creativity
• Basic recording awareness
• Performing with backing tracks
• Simple improvisation
• Creative musical exercises

• Musical storytelling

Chapter 4 - Graduation Showcase

• Dress rehearsal
• Final performance preparation
• Stage coordination
• Reflection and self-evaluation
• Showcase presentation

4. SONGWRITING & MUSIC PRODUCTION

This course is designed for students who wish to create original music. It introduces songwriting, melody writing, lyric writing, home recording, and beginner-level music production using a computer-based setup.

Course Objectives

• Write original songs.
• Understand song structure.
• Create melodies and chord progressions.
• Record basic demos.
• Learn the foundations of digital music production.

Chapter 1 - What Makes a Song?

     • Hook writing Chapter 3 - Lyric Writing
• Themes and emotions
• Storytelling in songs
• Rhyme and rhythm
     • Writing exercises Chapter 4 - Chords for Songwriters
• Popular chord progressions
• Emotional effect of chords
• Writing with four chords
     • Chord movement practice Chapter 5 - Introduction to Music Production
• Home studio basics

• Audio interface and microphone awareness
• Recording simple vocals and instruments
     • Session organization Chapter 6 - FL Studio Basics
• Interface overview
• Playlist and channel rack
• Piano roll basics
• Drum programming
     • Simple arrangement Chapter 7 - MIDI & Virtual Instruments
• MIDI keyboard basics
• Playing virtual instruments
• Editing MIDI notes
     • Layering sounds Chapter 8 - Build Your First Demo
• Recording a song idea
• Adding drums, chords, and melody
• Basic mixing awareness
• Exporting a demo track

5. MUSIC FOR SOUL (Working Professionals)

Music for Soul is a relaxed and non-competitive program created for working professionals, homemakers, entrepreneurs, and adults who want music as a source of joy, relaxation, and personal wellbeing.

Course Objectives

• Enjoy music without academic pressure.
• Reduce stress through singing and playing.
• Learn favorite songs in a simple way.
• Build confidence for informal singing and playing.
• Experience music as a daily wellness practice.

Chapter 3 - Easy Guitar / Keyboard Accompaniment

• Music for calmness

     • Personal music journal Chapter 5 - Weekend Music Circle
• Group singing
• Informal performance
• Listening and sharing
     • Community music activities Chapter 6 - Music as a Lifelong Companion

• Continuing practice independently
• Music for family and friends
• Joyful lifelong learning

These five programs together create a complete learning pathway for beginners, developing musicians, performers, creators, and adults seeking music for personal enrichment.
