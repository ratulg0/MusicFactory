THE MUSIC FACTORY by Archit
Daily Class Curriculum & Batch Structure
THE MUSIC FACTORY follows a 6-day teaching schedule (Monday to Saturday) with structured group batches for children and teenagers, and a separate evening batch for working professionals.
The academy is designed as a small-group music learning studio, allowing personal attention while creating a collaborative musical environment.

?

Batch Capacity
Each batch contains 5-6 students only. This limited strength allows:
• individual guidance,
• better discipline,
• more performance opportunities,
• and stronger student-teacher interaction.


?

Mixed-Instrument Learning Model
Unlike traditional music classes where all students learn the same instrument, every batch at THE MUSIC FACTORY is designed as a Musician's Batch.
A typical batch may include:
• 1-2 Vocalists
• 1-2 Guitar Students
• 1-2 Piano / Keyboard Students
• (Harmonium students may join the keyboard group when applicable.)
Students learn together while applying the same musical concept on their own instrument. Example
Musical Concept	Vocal Student Guitar Student Piano Student Major Scale	Sings the scale Plays the scale Plays the scale Rhythm PatternClaps / sings	Strums the pattern	Plays the rhythm Chord Progression	Sings over chords	Plays chords	Plays chords Dynamics Sings softly/loudly	Plays softly/loudly	Plays softly/loudly
This system helps students understand music as a common language, not as separate subjects.

Daily 60-Minute Class Curriculum (3 PM - 6 PM Batches)
1. Warm-Up (5 Minutes)
• Breathing exercises
• Vocal humming
• Finger stretches
• Hand coordination
2. Rhythm Training (10 Minutes)
• Clapping exercises
• Counting aloud
• Metronome practice
• Body percussion games
3. Theory of the Day (10 Minutes)
• Beat, rhythm, notes, scales, chords, or musical symbols
• Simple explanation suitable for children and teenagers
4. Instrument Application (20 Minutes)
Students practice the day's concept on their own instrument:
• Vocalists sing,
• Guitarists play,
• Pianists play,
• Harmonium students play.
The teacher rotates attention among students.
5. Ear Training & Listening (5 Minutes)
• High/low sound games
• Same/different exercises
• Rhythm imitation
• Melody echo
6. Song & Performance Practice (10 Minutes)
• Group singing
• Accompaniment practice
• Ensemble playing
• Stage confidence exercises

?

Working Professionals Batch (7:00 PM - 8:00 PM)
The evening batch is designed for adults who learn music for relaxation, self-expression, and personal enjoyment.
Focus Areas
• Singing favorite songs
• Easy guitar or keyboard accompaniment
• Breath and relaxation exercises
• Stress-relief music practice
• Informal group music sessions

Class Structure
• Gentle warm-up
• Song learning
• Simple accompaniment
• Listening and relaxation
• Group music sharing
No exams or performance pressure are included in this batch.


?

Monthly Performance Activity
At the end of every month, one class is conducted as a Music Circle, where students:
• perform for classmates,
• play together,
• sing together,
     • and gradually build confidence for stage performance. Parents may be invited for selected showcase sessions.


?

Learning Philosophy
Every batch is intentionally mixed with singers and instrumentalists so that students:
• learn to listen to each other,
• develop timing and coordination,
• experience ensemble music from the beginning,
• and grow as complete musicians rather than isolated instrumental learners.
This collaborative structure is one of the unique features of THE MUSIC FACTORY by Archit.
