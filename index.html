Our Philosophy

Music First.
Instrument Second.

Most academies teach you to play a song. We teach you to understand music. Our students learn rhythm, theory, ear training and expression before they ever touch an instrument — so when they do, they're ready to truly play.

Founded by Archit Bisht, The Music Factory is built on the belief that every student has a unique musical voice. Our role is to help them find it.

What We Offer

Five Programs. One Journey.

Five structured programs for beginners, developing musicians, performers, aspiring creators and working professionals — each focused on practical learning, musical understanding and personal expression.

Daily Curriculum

Small Batches.
Real Ensembles.

Every class is a 60-minute journey — warm-up, rhythm, theory, instrument time, ear training and ensemble practice — in tiny mixed batches of five to six students, three days a week.
