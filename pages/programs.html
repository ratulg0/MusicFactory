Stages in Detail

What Each Stage Covers

Term 1 · Months 1–4

Foundation

Students learn to feel rhythm, recognise melody and harmony, and develop listening habits and technique that every instrument shares.

Intermediate

Growth & Recognition

Outcomes, Assessment & Certification

Learning Outcomes

Each term has clear outcomes: rhythmic accuracy, musical understanding, expression and confident performance — measured continuously, not just at the end.

Assessments

Friendly term assessments, note practice habits and celebrate progress. Success is measured through understanding, confidence and expression.

Certificates

Students earn progressive certificates at each stage, and all Year One students celebrate completion at the graduation showcase — on a real stage.

Progression

A continuous journey: students always know where they are and what comes next — from first note to confident performer.
