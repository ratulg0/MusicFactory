# 13 Theme Rules

Maintain one consistent luxury dark theme.

Do not introduce alternate color schemes.

Images should use subtle dark overlays for readability.

Transitions: 200-400ms.

Animations: Elegant and restrained.

Avoid: - Neon colors - Excessive gradients - Flashing effects - Heavy
glassmorphism - Busy backgrounds

Acceptance Criteria: - Cohesive visual identity. - Premium feel on
desktop and mobile. - Consistent component styling across all pages.
