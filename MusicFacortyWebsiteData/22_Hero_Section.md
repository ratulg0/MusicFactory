# 22 Hero Section Specification

## Objective

Create an emotional first impression that encourages visitors to explore
or book a trial.

## Background

Use a full-screen image or looping muted video of students performing in
a warm studio environment.

Apply a dark overlay (50--70%) to ensure text readability.

## Content

Headline: Learn Music. Express Yourself. Own the Stage.

Supporting text: Introduce The Music Factory as a premium academy where
students learn music first and instruments second.

Primary CTA: Book Trial Class

Secondary CTA: Explore Programs

## Layout

-   Full viewport height.
-   Content aligned left on desktop.
-   Centered on mobile.
-   Scroll indicator at bottom.

## Animation

-   Hero fades in.
-   Heading slides upward.
-   Buttons fade with slight delay.
-   Background slow zoom (very subtle).

## Responsive

Desktop: split composition. Tablet: centered. Mobile: reduce heading
size and stack buttons.

## Acceptance

Headline remains readable on all devices. Buttons always visible above
fold.
