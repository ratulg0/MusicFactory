# 23 About Section Specification

## Purpose

Introduce parents and prospective students to The Music Factory as a
premium music academy focused on building confident musicians rather
than simply teaching instruments.

## Primary Message

Students learn **music first and instruments second**.

## Layout

Two-column desktop layout: - Left: full-height lifestyle image of
students learning. - Right: academy story.

Mobile: Image stacked above text.

## Content Blocks

-   Section title
-   Short academy introduction
-   Academy story
-   Mission
-   Vision
-   Motto
-   CTA: Learn More

## Visual Style

Dark background with warm gold accents. Generous whitespace. Image
corners rounded (20px).

## Animations

Image fades in from left. Content fades up sequentially.

## Accessibility

Images require descriptive alt text. Maintain reading order on mobile.

## Acceptance Criteria

Visitors understand the academy philosophy within 30 seconds.
