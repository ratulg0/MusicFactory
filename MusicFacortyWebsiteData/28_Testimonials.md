# 28 Testimonials Specification

## Purpose

Provide social proof for parents and prospective students.

## Layout

Three testimonial cards on desktop. Carousel on tablet/mobile.

## Card Content

-   Student or Parent name
-   Optional student age
-   Short quote
-   Rating (5 stars)
-   Optional profile photo

## Design

Dark cards Gold quotation mark Cream text Soft shadow Rounded corners

## Animation

Cards fade upward on scroll. Carousel slides smoothly with touch
support.

## CTA Below Section

'Join our next batch and start your musical journey.'

## Accessibility

Carousel controls keyboard accessible.
