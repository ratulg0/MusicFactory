# 21 Navigation Specification

## Structure

Logo \| Home \| About \| Programs \| Gallery \| Contact \| Book Trial

## Behavior

-   Transparent over hero.
-   Turns solid black after scrolling 80px.
-   Sticky at top.
-   Smooth transition (250ms).

## Mobile

-   Hamburger menu.
-   Full-screen slide-out panel.
-   Body scroll locked when menu is open.

## Styling

Height: 80px desktop, 68px mobile. Logo left, CTA button right. Gold
underline for active page.

## Accessibility

-   aria-label for menu button.
-   ESC closes menu.
-   Focus trapped while mobile menu is open.

## Acceptance

Navigation works with mouse, keyboard, and touch.
