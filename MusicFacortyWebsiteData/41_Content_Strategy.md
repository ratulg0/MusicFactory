# 41 Content Strategy

## Purpose

Every page should educate, inspire confidence and guide visitors toward
booking a trial class.

## Primary Audience

Parents of children (6--18), teenagers, aspiring musicians.

## Tone

Professional, warm, encouraging, premium, authentic.

## Content Priority

1.  Clear headline
2.  Emotional value
3.  Academy credibility
4.  Curriculum
5.  Social proof
6.  Strong CTA

## Rules

-   Never use lorem ipsum.
-   Use concise paragraphs.
-   Prefer active voice.
-   Every section ends with a logical next action.
