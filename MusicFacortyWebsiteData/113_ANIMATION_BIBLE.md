# Animation Bible

## Philosophy

Motion supports content, never distracts.

## Durations

Fast 200ms Normal 300ms Slow 500ms

## Easing

ease-out for entrances ease-in-out for hover

## Scroll

Fade Up Fade Left Fade Right Zoom

## Hover

Buttons: translateY(-2px) Cards: translateY(-8px) Images: scale(1.05)

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
