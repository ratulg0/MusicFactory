# 20 Home Page Specification

## Purpose

The Home page is the primary marketing page. It must immediately
communicate that The Music Factory is a premium music academy focused on
confidence, musicianship, and performance.

## User Goals

-   Understand what the academy offers.
-   Trust the academy.
-   Explore programs.
-   Book a trial class.

## Page Flow

1.  Transparent Navigation
2.  Hero
3.  Academy Introduction
4.  Philosophy
5.  Programs Preview
6.  Why Choose Us
7.  Performance Culture
8.  Gallery Preview
9.  Testimonials
10. Final CTA
11. Footer

## Layout Rules

-   Max width: 1280px
-   Alternate dark section backgrounds.
-   Large vertical spacing (96--120px desktop).
-   Use asymmetrical layouts where suitable.
-   Every section has one primary CTA.

## Accessibility

-   One H1 only.
-   Semantic landmarks.
-   Keyboard accessible navigation.
-   Meaningful alt text.

## Acceptance Criteria

-   Lighthouse \>90.
-   Fully responsive.
-   No layout shifts.
-   Loads quickly on mobile.
