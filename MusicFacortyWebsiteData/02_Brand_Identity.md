# 02 Brand Identity

Brand Name: THE MUSIC FACTORY by Archit

Primary Message: Learn Music. Express Yourself. Own the Stage.

Brand Personality

-   Premium
-   Artistic
-   Modern
-   Professional
-   Friendly
-   Inspirational

The site should feel closer to a premium photography portfolio than a
traditional coaching website.

Never use loud gradients or cartoon styling.

Photography is the hero of the design.
